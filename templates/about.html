About This Tool

About Digital Shadow

A transparent, production-grade privacy risk analyzer. Digital Shadow helps individuals understand what personal information they expose online — and how to reduce their digital footprint.

🛡

100% Local Analysis

All analysis runs server-side with no outbound requests. Your submitted data is never sent to third-party services, APIs, or analytics platforms.

🔍

Rule-Based Detection

Digital Shadow uses 15+ deterministic, explainable rules to detect exposure patterns — no machine learning, no black boxes. Every finding includes its evidence and reasoning.

📊

Heuristic Risk Scoring

A 0–100 risk score is computed from weighted severity contributions. Critical findings carry more weight than low-severity ones, with deduplication to prevent score inflation.

📄

Document Metadata

Upload a PDF or DOCX file to extract embedded metadata: author name, software used, creation date, and revision history. Files are deleted immediately after processing.

🔗

URL Structure Analysis

Profile and website URLs are parsed locally to detect HTTPS issues, embedded credentials, tracking parameters, IP-based hostnames, and punycode deception — without making any requests.

💾

Report Export

Download your analysis report as JSON or CSV. Reports are generated client-side and contain only what you submitted and the findings detected.

Detection Coverage

What Digital Shadow Detects

Six exposure categories, each with multiple detection rules.

📧
Identity & Contact

Email addresses, phone numbers, date of birth, and physical address patterns.

🔗
URL & Profile Links

HTTPS enforcement, credentials in URLs, tracking parameters, and IP-based hostnames.

👤
Username Reuse

Detection of identical usernames across multiple platforms, enabling cross-platform correlation.

🔑
Sensitive Keywords

Filenames and text patterns suggesting passwords, credentials, private keys, or financial data.

🔗
Combination Risks

Name + contact, name + address, and multi-attribute combinations that amplify exposure risk.

📄
Document Metadata

Author identity, editing software, revision history, and sensitive filename patterns in uploaded documents.
