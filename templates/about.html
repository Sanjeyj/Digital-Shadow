About Digital Shadow

Digital Shadow explores personal data exposure, privacy risk analysis, and practical cybersecurity education.

🎯

The Problem

People routinely share usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and profile links without understanding how these individually ordinary pieces of data combine to create a uniquely identifying profile — a "digital shadow."

🔬

The Approach

Digital Shadow uses deterministic, rule-based analysis to evaluate information you voluntarily supply. Every finding includes evidence, an explanation, a consequence, and a practical mitigation step — making the analysis transparent and educational.

🏗

Technology

Built with Python Flask on the backend, Vanilla JavaScript and CSS3 on the frontend, SQLite for local persistence, and Chart.js for visualizations. No machine learning, no external APIs, no trackers.

🔒

Privacy First

Analysis runs entirely on the local server. No submitted data is sent to third parties. URLs are analyzed structurally — no outbound HTTP requests are made. Uploaded documents are deleted immediately after metadata extraction.
